// about

I work at both ends of the stack —
architecture on one side, deployment on the other.

I'm a Data Science graduate (B.Tech, Manipal University Jaipur, 2025) who spent the last two-and-a-half years mostly refusing to stay in one lane: computational oncology, biomedical AI, geospatial ML, generative audio. What ties it together is a habit of pulling architectures apart to first principles — Transformers, ResNets, GCNs, GATs, Seq2Seq — and then rebuilding them for whatever the domain actually needs, whether that's a positional encoding that understands music theory or a PDE constraint that understands tumor growth.

The other half of the job is making sure the model survives contact with production: Docker, FastAPI, CI/CD, dynamic batching, GPU-resident inference, confidence calibration, graceful degradation when one stage of a pipeline fails. NeuroSight below is the clearest example of both halves at once.
